# PayRex Merchant Onboarding Note

PayRex's public guidance states that Sandbox access can be used while a merchant prepares onboarding documents, but it cannot process real payments until merchant activation/KYM is approved. The merchant application is tied to the legal business receiving settlement, and the public guidance lists legal business information, registration details, tax information, valid IDs, and a settlement bank account among the expected requirements.

This project therefore remains in **Sandbox/test mode** until the owner completes PayRex merchant activation. No claim should be made in buyer-facing content that unregistered-business test transactions are real payments or that money will settle to a personal GCash wallet.
